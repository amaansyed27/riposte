Credits
ScoreBuilt from three orchestral pieces by Luis_Humanoide, used under the Pixabay Content License and edited into loops:
Secrets of the Jedi (the breath between bouts, and its swell when a master falls) · The Path of the Force (the menu theme) · Star Wars Style Battle Music (bouts) · Temple of the Dark Side (masters).
Adaptive mixThe beds are split live into low, mid and high layers that follow the fight: bolts in the air, orbs alive, your streak, your last guard. Timpani, low brass, cymbal swells and the four-note horn call are synthesized here and land on what you do.
SoundEvery effect — the hum, ignition, bolts, impacts, rain, thunder, the orbs’ chatter — is synthesized in your browser. No samples.
